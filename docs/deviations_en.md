## P4 implementation clarifications

| Date | Basis | Decision and rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11-25 | G3 4.1 and 4.7, S9, S10 | The pooled comparison of persistently versus transiently elevated BP for the 7-day return was the sole primary contrast. Models used independent working correlation and clustering by center and patient. The pooled and site-specific 7- and 30-day models used centered age and its square. The MC-MED model included only age and sex. A joint Wald test evaluated phenotype-by-center interaction. The fixed-effect synthesis used the inverse variance of site-specific log RRs. When diagnostics were irregular, we checked a patient-clustered Poisson GLM with the same covariates. |
| 2026-11-25 | G3 4.2, S10 | The last-SBP restricted cubic spline was scaled per 10 mmHg. The fit-sample knots were the 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles. The median was the reference, and the plotted domain spanned the 2.5th to 97.5th percentiles. The model included the eligible return cohort. |
| 2026-11-25 | G3 4.3, S3.2, S7.2 | The 30-day analysis used the same model as the primary outcome. Landmark phenotype required a reading in the interval (0, 60] minutes. The final eligible reading distinguished transiently elevated and persistently elevated, and normal on arrival also required a reading in the interval. The numerical arrival-SBP model included all risk-set visits with arrival BP. The broader admission rule rebuilt the risk set separately. G2 approved andexanet, and no matching orders occurred. We first audited all eligible linked admissions, then fit crude and linear age-adjusted procedure models within completed BP cohort visits. |
| 2026-11-25 | G3 4.4, S5.4 | Exploratory SBP burden tertiles used MC-MED return-cohort quantiles, retaining observations tied at a boundary in the same group. The P subtype and SBP burden models reported crude risk ratios with patient-clustered robust variance. |
| 2026-11-25 | G3 4.5, S11 | First visits were selected in the S3.2-eligible population before checking return-cohort eligibility. The primary-diagnosis-only definition rebuilt episodes, index and returns. The expanded discharge group included leaving without being seen, against medical advice, or after observation. Elopement was outside that scenario. Return timing still used recorded departure. The early final-reading scenario took the last reading at or before 30 minutes before departure and allowed the first reading to serve as the final reading. The S11-12 medication-list restriction applied to MC-MED, while S11-16 applied to both centers. S11-13 and S11-17 replaced the hypertension indicator at the corresponding center. S11-4 removed documented antithrombotic use, making that variable structurally constant and unestimable. |
| 2026-11-25 | S10 BP-cohort selection, G3 4.1 | Availability comparisons used all index visits and the direct-home discharge candidates. IPW targeted direct-home discharges among new episodes. Age and age squared, sex, antithrombotic use, documented hypertension, triage acuity and center modeled BP cohort availability. We used untruncated inverse predicted probabilities. The patient-clustered variance of the weighted GEE was conditional on estimated weights. |

| 2026-11-25 | G4 5.1 and 5.4, S8.2-1, S9-S10 | Among BP cohort visits with elevated arrival BP, the treatment model used peak SBP before the first dose for treated visits and the ED-stay peak for untreated visits. | 2026-11-25 | G4 5.2 and 5.5, S8.2-2, S12 | Follow-up for treated visits began at the first dose. Baseline BP was the last reading in the preceding 60 minutes, including the dose timestamp. The matched reading set the control anchor. Ranking used absolute SBP difference followed by absolute elapsed-time difference. A control could contribute once per set. A fixed seed resolved ties. Both follow-up windows included their bounds. Equidistant readings were resolved in favor of the earlier time, then P2 source priority. Each time point required readings for the treated visit and at least one control. |
| 2026-11-25 | G4 5.2 and 5.5, S8.2-2, S12 | We averaged available controls within a matched set and then averaged across sets with equal weight. A sensitivity analysis excluded class B-treated controls and repeated matching. |
| 2026-11-25 | G4 5.3 and 5.4, S8.2-3, S11-15 | The return cohort used visit counts. Site-specific and pooled 7-day crude RRs and two-sided Fisher exact tests were reported. The pooled crude estimate used combined counts from both centers. |
